Interpolation and polynomials, in pure Go.
Measured against SciPy and NumPy.

go-approx is the approximation layer of the Go scientific stack: interpolation and splines with the semantics of scipy.interpolate, and polynomials with those of numpy.polyfit, numpy.roots and numpy.polynomial. Both libraries run SciPy and NumPy as oracles from their tests and record where they differ. No cgo.

Why go-approx

Approximation, with the reference named.

Each Go function names the SciPy, NumPy or MATLAB call it corresponds to, and the deliberate differences are listed in each library's README.

📈

Interpolators

interp1d kinds, cubic splines with not-a-knot, natural, clamped and periodic conditions, Hermite, PCHIP, Akima and makima.

〰️

B-splines

make_interp_spline of any degree, make_lsq_spline, FITPACK smoothing splines, design matrices, derivatives, antiderivatives and integrals.

▦

Grids

Regular-grid interpolation (linear to quintic), tensor-product splines and bicubic interpolation.

x²

Polynomials

Evaluation by Horner or compensated Horner, arithmetic, derivatives, integrals, companion-matrix roots.

📉

Fits

Polyfit with weights, rcond and covariance, and fits in the Chebyshev, Legendre, Hermite, HermiteE and Laguerre bases.

📦

Pure Go, CGO=0

No cgo. poly's CI runs Linux, macOS, Windows, arm64, and qemu riscv64, loong64, ppc64le, s390x and arm.

🧭

Planned

Chebyshev approximation and AAA rational approximation are not written yet.

⚖️

BSD-3-Clause

Every repository of the organization. Ported code keeps its attribution in place.
